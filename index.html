The Basics

Why How to Lock Your Credit File Matters More Than Ever in 2025


A credit file lock explicitly revokes access to your credit reports. When you apply for a loan, a mortgage, or a new credit card, the lender checks your file at Equifax, Experian, or TransUnion. If the file is locked, the inquiry is blocked, and the application is automatically denied.


Because data brokers continuously collect and monetize personal data, the chances of your information appearing on the dark web are incredibly high. A credit lock acts as a digital deadbolt against the fallout of these corporate data breaches.


Interactive: Credit Lock vs Credit Freeze explainer


People often search for "experian credit file lock vs freeze" or "credit file lock vs freeze". While they accomplish the same goal—blocking lenders from viewing your report—they differ in legal protections and convenience.













The Convenience Option


A Credit Lock is a product offered by the credit bureaus. It is often tied to an app, allowing you to lock and unlock your credit file instantly with a single tap or swipe. Some bureaus offer this for free (like TransUnion's TrueIdentity), while others bundle it into premium monthly subscriptions.


    

  • Speed: Instant unlock via smartphone app.
    • 

  • Cost: Can incur monthly fees depending on the bureau.
    • 

  • Legal Backing: Governed by the terms of service of the bureau; lacks federal guarantee against financial loss if the lock fails.
    • 







The Federal Standard


A Credit Freeze (or Security Freeze) is a federally mandated right. By law, it is completely free to place and lift at all three major bureaus. It provides stronger legal protections in the event of fraud.


    

  • Speed: May take up to 24-48 hours to lift, though many online requests process within an hour.
    • 

  • Cost: 100% Free by federal law.
    • 

  • Legal Backing: Guaranteed by federal law. If someone fraudulently opens an account while a freeze is active, you are legally protected from liability.
    •

How do I lock my credit file?



You must visit the websites for Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion individually. Create an account on each platform and toggle the "Credit Lock" or "Security Freeze" option to active.




Does locking my credit file hurt my credit score?



No. Placing a lock or a freeze on your credit file has absolutely zero impact on your credit score. Your existing creditors will still update your payment history normally.




Can i lock my experian credit report for free?



Yes. While Experian pushes a paid "CreditLock" product, you can request a "Security Freeze" through their website at absolutely no cost. By federal law, a freeze is always free.




Does experian credit lock lock all three bureaus?



No. Experian, Equifax, and TransUnion are separate competing companies. Locking your file at Experian does not lock the other two. You must lock all three manually.




How can I find hidden locked files on my PC?



If you used a tool like Folder Lock to secure your downloaded credit reports locally, open the Folder Lock application and enter your master password. The files are securely hidden from the OS and cannot be found via standard search.
